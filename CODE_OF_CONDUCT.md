# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique behavior and code without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information and unwelcome sexual content are not accepted.

Keep reports relevant to the project. Do not post credentials, private logs or media you do not have permission to share. Respect the maintainer's scope and the time of other contributors.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove harmful content and restrict repeated abusive participation. Report concerns privately to security@lucaslepajollec.com, with only the context needed to investigate. Reports are handled privately where practical; no response-time guarantee is made.
